I’m Emma, an artificial intelligence engineer based in Madrid, with a background at Universidad Pontificia Comillas. My work explores how machines learn, reason, and turn complex information into something useful.

From graph neural networks and uncertainty-aware NLP to AI agents, I enjoy connecting rigorous experimentation with practical applications. Beyond code, volunteering as an English teacher in Peru has shaped the human perspective I bring to my work.
